Sonder Group

Brand Identity • Social Media • Web Design • Motion

Form Study

Sonder Group was developed as a multi-touchpoint brand system across logo animation, brochure design, social media visuals, and digital-facing brand communication. The form study focused on translating one identity across motion, print, and social formats.

Concept

The concept positions Sonder Group as a bold digital solutions brand combining technology, creativity, consulting, and strategic communication. The visual identity needed to feel innovative, confident, and commercially credible while still being adaptable for social media and client-facing material.

Art Direction

The art direction uses a high-contrast orange, black, white, and grey palette, paired with futuristic typography and editorial imagery. The system is direct, motivational, and social-first, using bold overlays and strong hierarchy to guide attention quickly.

Design

The final design outcome extends the brand across brochure design, animated logo work, campaign visuals, and educational carousel content. This shows how one identity can remain consistent while adapting across different communication needs.
