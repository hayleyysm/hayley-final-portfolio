MALAIKA

BRAND IDENTITY • STRATEGY • LOGO DESIGN • SOCIAL MEDIA

Form Study

The form study investigated multiple visual approaches before arriving at the final identity. Iterations explored abstraction, symbolism, playfulness, and recognisable floral motifs to communicate warmth and care.

Concept

The concept centred on positioning Malaika as a contemporary wellness brand. Strategic messaging focused on empowerment, trust, and authentic connection with its audience.

Art Direction

The art direction paired soft neutrals with vibrant accents to balance professionalism and approachability. Presentation materials introduced organic textures and lifestyle imagery to reinforce the brand personality.

Design

The final outcome brought together identity, strategy, and communication design into a cohesive system. The project demonstrates how visual consistency can support both internal planning and outward-facing brand experiences.
