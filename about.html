Biography

I’m Hayley Simone Moonsamy, a third-year Digital Arts student specialising in interactive media, visual identity, web design, and creative problem-solving.

My work explores how design can shape clear, engaging, and meaningful user experiences across branding, digital platforms, and interactive projects.

This portfolio brings together selected work across brand identity, interaction design, visual communication, and digital media practice.
